Andes Retail Commercial Performance Analysis

Power BI dashboard and business analysis project focused on the commercial performance of Andes Retail Group during 2024–2025.

This project analyzes sales, profitability, customer segments, product categories, countries, regions, and temporal business patterns to support executive decision-making through a clear and interactive dashboard.

Project Objective

Andes Retail Group operates across Peru, Chile, and Colombia, selling products in four main categories: Electronics, Clothing, Sports, and Home.

The company needed a consolidated view of its transactional data to understand business performance and identify commercial opportunities. The objective of this project was to transform raw sales data into a clear Power BI dashboard that supports strategic decision-making.

Business Questions

The dashboard was designed to answer questions such as:

How did total revenue evolve between 2024 and 2025?

Which customer segments contribute the most revenue and profit?

Which product categories have the greatest business impact?

Are there relevant differences between countries or regions?

What temporal patterns can be observed across the year?

Where are the main opportunities for commercial improvement?

Dashboard Structure

The dashboard was designed with two main views:

1. Executive Overview

This view provides a quick reading of the overall business performance. It includes key performance indicators and high-level visuals related to:

Total revenue

Total profit

Profit margin

Units sold

Total orders

Unique customers

Revenue evolution by year

Revenue by product category

Revenue by country

2. Detailed Analysis

This view supports deeper exploration and diagnosis. It includes visuals focused on:

Revenue and profit by customer segment

Profit by product category

Revenue by region

Summary table by country, region, category, and customer segment

The objective of this second view is to help stakeholders identify where differences, patterns, and opportunities are concentrated.

Key Findings

The approved project documentation highlights the following business findings:

Andes Retail Group generated approximately 5.53 million in total revenue.

Total profit was approximately 1.94 million.

Profit margin was close to 35.10%.

Revenue decreased between 2024 and 2025, requiring commercial monitoring.

The Premium segment contributed the highest revenue and profit.

Sports and Electronics showed strong profitability performance.

The North region led in revenue, while Central and South remained close.

Strategic Interpretation

The dashboard suggests that Andes Retail Group should monitor the revenue decrease observed between 2024 and 2025 while reinforcing actions in the strongest commercial segments and categories.

Priority areas include:

Strengthening commercial actions for the Premium segment.

Monitoring regional performance to identify differences between markets.

Reinforcing high-performing categories such as Sports and Electronics.

Using the detailed view to explore opportunities by country, region, category, and customer segment.
